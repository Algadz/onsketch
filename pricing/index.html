Questions

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancel from Manage subscription (account menu or this page). Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then your account goes back to Free.

What happens to my sketches if I stop Pro?

Nothing is deleted. You keep all your sketches and can open and edit them. Private sketches stay private. On the free plan you can't add a new sketch while you have 3 or more, and you can't make a sketch private.

Which exports are free?

On every plan: PNG, SVG and copy to the clipboard for slides and documents, and printing or PDF at a true scale. DXF, to and from CAD, CAM and laser cutters at real size, is part of OnSketch Pro.

Who handles the payment?

Payments are handled by Link, Stripe's checkout, as our reseller (merchant of record): it takes the card payment (Apple Pay and Google Pay where your device offers them), pays the sales tax or VAT of your country (included in the price), and sends the receipts and invoices. Your statement shows LINK.COM* ONSKETCH.IO. OnSketch never sees your card details.

Is there a refund?

If something went wrong, write to us through “Report a problem” in the editor and we'll sort it out.
