Questions

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancel from Manage subscription (account menu or this page). Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then your account goes back to Free.

What happens to my sketches if I stop Pro?

Nothing is deleted. You keep all your sketches and can open and edit them. Private sketches stay private. On the free plan you can't add a new sketch while you have 3 or more, and you can't make a sketch private.

Who handles the payment?

Payments go through Lemon Squeezy, our reseller: it takes the card or PayPal payment, adds the sales tax or VAT of your country and sends the invoices. OnSketch never sees your card details.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Write to us through “Report a problem” in the editor and we'll switch it, or cancel and subscribe again with the other period when the current one ends.

Is there a refund?

If something went wrong, write to us through “Report a problem” in the editor and we'll sort it out.
