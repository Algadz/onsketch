OnSketch in Claude and ChatGPT

Beta

Describe what you want drawn to your AI assistant. It draws the sketch to scale, saves it in your OnSketch account and gives you a link. Open it to drag shapes, change dimensions, or export it to SVG, PNG, PDF or DXF.

Draw a 60 × 30 mm mounting plate with a rounded right end and two 6 mm holes 40 mm apart, in OnSketch.

Connect it

You need an OnSketch account (free) and the connector address:

https://rwmzpzaqijgdfsjmpqbd.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp

Claude (claude.ai, desktop and mobile apps): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it OnSketch, paste the address, then Connect and sign in to OnSketch.

ChatGPT: Settings → Apps & Connectors → turn on Developer mode under Advanced, then Create. Name it OnSketch, paste the address, choose OAuth, and sign in to OnSketch.

Custom connectors depend on your assistant's plan, and both apps move these settings from time to time: if the steps above don't match, search their help for "custom connector".

What the assistant can do

  • Create sketches in your account: lines, rectangles, polylines, circles, arcs and points, with relations (parallel, tangent, symmetric…) and dimensions you can edit afterwards.
  • Read your sketches and change them. Each change is kept in the sketch's version history.
  • List your sketches.

It can't delete sketches, change whether a sketch is public, or touch your profile, likes or plan. On the free plan sketches are public in the library, so the assistant asks you before saving one. Private sketches need OnSketch Pro.

Sketches that hold ink, text, fills, curves or formulas can be read but not changed by the assistant (it would lose them): edit those in OnSketch.

Disconnect

Remove the connector in your assistant's settings. What the assistant sees and keeps is covered by its own privacy policy; what OnSketch stores is in ours.
