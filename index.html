Pretty or precise. Why pick one?

When you present an idea, the first question is often "does it fit?". Most tools make you choose between a nice picture and a right answer.

Slides and drawing apps

Quick and good-looking, but nothing is to scale. The idea falls apart at the first measurement.

Pretty, not precise

CAD software

Exact, but heavy to learn and slow to start, and the drawings are hard to show to people who don't use CAD.

Precise, not presentable

OnSketch

Draw freely, give shapes their real dimensions, then colour them like a painting. One visual that is both clear and correct.

A real sketch engine underneath.

Easy on the surface, with the precision of a CAD sketcher when you want it.

Geometric constraints

Horizontal, parallel, tangent, equal, coincident and more keep your shapes the way you mean them as you edit.

Line colour shows how much is pinned down: free to move, fully defined, conflicting.

Equations

Every dimension has a name. Link them with formulas like =width / 2 or =D1 + 50mm and change a whole concept by editing one value.

No silent fudging

If dimensions contradict each other, OnSketch tells you and leaves your shapes alone instead of bending them into an approximate fit.

Your files, your machine

No cloud account needed. Export a project as a JSON file to keep, back up, and import again later.
