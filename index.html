In the editor

CAD precision underneath. Paint on top.

Dimensions in the units you think in

Type 2.4m, 35cm, 4in or 1in + 4mm. The shape resizes to match.

Fill any closed area

Click inside a shape to colour it. Move or resize the shape and the colour stays where it belongs.

Freehand ink and highlights

Circle a detail, mark a path, add a note. Ink sits on its layer and never disturbs the geometry.

Layers

Walls, furniture and notes on separate layers, each with its own visibility, opacity and order.

Named dimensions and equations

Link sizes with formulas like =width / 2 and change a whole concept by editing one value.

Nothing to install

Runs in the browser, saves as you go, and exports your project as a file you keep.

Under the hood

A real sketch engine, honest about your numbers.

Geometric constraints keep shapes the way you mean them. Line colour tells you, at a glance, how much of the drawing is pinned down.

Free to moveSome sizes or positions are still open.
Fully definedEvery size is set. Nothing moves by accident.
ConflictingTwo dimensions disagree. You're told; shapes are not bent to fit.
ConstructionReference lines that guide the drawing but aren't part of it.
  • RelationsHorizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, tangent, equal, coincident, midpoint, symmetric.
  • EquationsEvery dimension has a name (D1, D2…). Rename it, reference it, build on it.
  • UnitsTyped values take units and arithmetic. Everything is stored in millimetres and degrees.
  • Your filesProjects save in the browser as you work and export as JSON you can back up and reopen anywhere.
